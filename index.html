AutoPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

AutoPost ("we", "us") is a tool that creates videos and images and publishes them to social media accounts that users connect themselves. This policy explains what data AutoPost handles and why.

Data we collect

  • Account details: the email address you sign up with.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect Instagram, YouTube or another platform, we store the account ID, username and the access token the platform gives us. We use them only to publish the posts you create or schedule in AutoPost and to show their status.
  • Content you create: scripts, captions, images, videos and schedules you make in AutoPost.

How we use it

Only to run AutoPost for you: generating content, publishing it to the accounts you connected, at the times you choose. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except the platforms you publish to and the service providers that host AutoPost (Supabase for database and storage).

Instagram and Meta data

With your permission (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish) AutoPost reads your Instagram account ID and username and publishes Reels, Stories and posts you created in AutoPost. We do not read your messages, followers or other people's data.

Storage and security

Data is stored on Supabase servers with access restricted to your account. Access tokens are stored server-side and never shown in the app.

Keeping and deleting data

You can remove any connected social account in AutoPost at any time, which deletes its stored token. To delete all your data, follow the steps on our data deletion page.
